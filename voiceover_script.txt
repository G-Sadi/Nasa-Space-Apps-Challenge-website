We've been cooking something special for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. Meet Field Shift, built by team ASL_SPECTRA. Farmers like Sheikh Siraj face unpredictable rainfall, heat waves, and shifting soil moisture. What should they grow next? Field Shift connects live NASA SMAP, MODIS, and ECOSTRESS satellite data into a Four-Zone Radar and a 3D Field Digital Twin. With our Climate Scenario Simulator, Crop Rotation Planner, and bilingual Farmer Buddy AI, farmers choose high-yield crops with confidence. ASL_SPECTRA — adapting farms with NASA data.
